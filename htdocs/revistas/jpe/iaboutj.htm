Basic information

The Jornal de Políticas Educacionais (JPE) is an online journal, continuous flow, open access, and does not establish any charge for submission or publication. It publishes articles in Portuguese, English or Spanish.

It is organized and maintained by researchers from the Nucleus of Educational Policies – NuPE, from the Education Sector of UFPR, since 2007, evaluated by Qualis CAPES 2017-2020 as A2. ISSN 1981-1969

Its purpose is to publish unpublished articles in the area of Educational Policies, covering themes that involve the analysis of policies at all levels and modalities of teaching, as well as the evaluation of educational policies, educational planning and management, education financing, comparative education, among others. other themes in this field of knowledge.

 

Frequency

This journal publishes articles in a continuous publication system (continuous flow).
