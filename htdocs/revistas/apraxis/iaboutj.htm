Focus and scope

Revista Práxis Educacional is a quarterly electronic journal from the Graduate Program in Education (PPGEd), from the State University of Southwest Bahia (Uesb). Publishes unpublished articles resulting from scientific research, in addition to book reviews. Its main objective is to disseminate research and studies linked to the field of education, developed by researchers from different educational contexts in Brazil and abroad. The magazine does not charge any fee for the publication of the texts.
