Basic Information

Cadernos de Pesquisa, is a scientific journal, published since 1971 by Fundação Carlos Chagas, a recognized center in educational research in the country.

The journal has as its main goal the publication of academic research in education, in order to stimulate the exchange of information and the discussion of theoretical and methodological issues, dealing with relationships between education and broader social questions, guidelines for public policies, educational evaluation, and subjects such as ethnical-racial, gender and family. Priority is given to the publication of studies conducted in Brazil and to an interdisciplinary viewpoint.

Cadernos de Pesquisa was a quarterly journal until 1996. Since 1997 it is published three times a year. Between 1979 and 1998, the journal was co-edited by Cortez Editora, and from 1999 by Editora Autores Associados.

Those publishing houses have been in charge of the commercial distribution of the journal.

The abbreviated title, Cad. Pesqui., should be used in bibliographies, footnotes and bibliographcal references and strips.
