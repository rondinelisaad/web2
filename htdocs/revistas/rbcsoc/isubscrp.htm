SUBSCRIPTION

 

Subscription prices of the printed version of the Revista Brasileira de Ciências Sociais (one volume of three issues, published on February, June and October) are:
  • National: R$ 50,00
  • International: US$ 70,00.

Single issues can be purchased according to availability at the cost:

  • Issues 21, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 35 to 44, 46 to 49: R$ 10,00
  • Issues 50 and 51: R$ 18,50

To subscribe to the journal, one of the following ways can be chosen:

  • Cheque in the name of ANPOCS, accompanied by the subscriber’s name and full address (including postal code, city, state, telephone, fax, e-mail etc.). A payment receipt will be sent by mail, and the issues just after their release.
  • Deposit in the Banco do Brasil (agency 3559-9, account number 15801-1). The deposit receipt should be sent to the journal accompanied by the subscriber’s name and full address (including postal code, city, state, telephone, fax, e-mail etc.). A payment receipt will be sent by mail, and the issues just after their release.
