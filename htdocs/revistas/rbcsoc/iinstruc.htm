Scope and Editorial Policy

The Revista Brasileira de Ciências Sociais publishes articles and reviews in the Social Sciences fields.  The journal has consolidated its performance as one of the main Brazilian publications in the area of social sciences, reaching a wide thematic and conceptual diversity. It publishes not only Brazilian authors, but also contributions of renowned foreigner social scientists.

Spontaneous presentations of any contributions automatically implicate the full cession of the copyright to ANPOCS. The journal will not return the originals to authors.

 

 

Preparation of Originals

Two hard copies of originals should be presented, typed double-spaced with wide margins. Articles should not exceed 30 pages (20 lines each), or six thousand words. Book reviews should not exceed 7 pages.

Articles should include an abstract and keywords, as well as author identification (institution, position, fields of interest, recent publications etc.).

Authors whose works are accepted for publication should send a diskette (IBM-PC format) containing the article, preferably prepared in Word for Windows, accompanied by a printed copy. Articles should be presented as follows:

  • Tables, maps etc. should be prepared in separate files; texts should clear indicate the position in which illustrations should be set.
  • Citations should follow the model (Author, date), or (Author, date, page). Examples: (Jaguaribe, 1962) or (Jaguaribe, 1962, p. 35). If there are more than one work of the same author, a letter following the year should be used to differentiate them: (Adorno, 1975a), (Adorno, 1975b) etc.
  • Footnotes should only contain complementary and substantial information with no more than three lines.
  • Bibliographical references should appear at the end of the article, in alphabetical order.

Bibliographical references

Book: author’s last name (in capital letters) / COMMA / followed by the author’s first name / FULL STOP / date within parentheses / COMMA / title of the work in italic / FULL STOP / translator’s name / FULL STOP / edition, if other than the first / COMMA / place of publication / COMMA / publisher / FULL STOP. Example:

SACHS, Ignacy. (1986), Ecodesenvolvimento, crescer sem destruir. Tradução de Eneida Cidade Araújo. 2a. edição, São Paulo, Vértice.

Article: author’s last name (in capital letters) / COMMA / followed by the author’s first name / FULL STOP / date within parentheses / COMMA / "title of the work within quotation marks" / FULL STOP / title of the periodical in italic / COMMA / volume number / COMMA / issue number / COLON / page numbers. Example:

REIS, Elisa. (1982), "Elites agrárias, state-building e autoritarismo". Dados, 25, 3: 275-96.

Chapter in book: author’s last name (in capital letters) / COMMA / followed by the author’s first name / FULL STOP / date within parentheses / COMMA / "title of the chapter within quotation marks" / COMMA / the word in (in italic) / initials of the first names followed by the last name of the editors / COMMA / title of the book in italic / COMMA / place of publication / COMMA / publisher / FULL STOP. Example:

ABRANCHES, Sérgio Henrique. (1987), "Governo, empresa estatal e política siderúrgica: 1930-1975", in O.B. Lima & S.H. Abranches (org.), As origens da crise, São Paulo, Iuperj / Vértice.

Theses: author’s last name (in capital letters) / COMMA / followed by the author’s first name / FULL STOP / date within parentheses / COMMA / title of the thesis in italic / FULL STOP / academic degree / COMMA / institution where the work was presented / COMMA / type of printing (typewritten or digitalized) / FULL STOP. Example:

SGUIZZARDI, Eunice Helena. (1986), O estruturalismo de Piaget: subsídios para a determinação de um lugar comum para a Ciência e a Arquitetura. Tese de mestrado. Fundação Escola de Sociologia e Política de São Paulo, datilo.
