Author guidelines

REnCiMa, eISSN 2179-426X, is edited by the Graduate Program in Science and Mathematics Education of the Cruzeiro do Sul University. It publishes original and unpublished articles, double-blind arbitrated, whose theoretical and analytical basis is the knowledge produced in the field of Science and Mathematics Education. It publishes productions that present research outcomes, theoretical essays and critical analysis of pedagogical practices, which address epistemological, philosophical, didactic, methodological and / or conceptual aspects of Sciences and their objects of knowledge. The editorial project prioritizes texts in Portuguese, Spanish or English. Also, at the Editorial Board's discretion, special issues are published to review areas and / or analyze issues arising from educational research. Submission takes place as an ongoing flow and the publication of manuscripts is free of cost. LOCKSS and CLOKSS are used to ensure secure and permanent archiving of the magazine's cache.
Regarding the preparation of the article and its submission, the following must be observed:

  • The article must have a maximum of four authors.
  • At least one of the authors must have a doctorate and / or act as a professor in a graduate program in the area of ​​Science and Mathematics Education or related areas.
  • Grammatical, syntactic, orthographic and bibliographic correction is the responsibility of the authors; if approved, the article must be submitted to a review by a professional.
  • Correct writing, creativity in the theoretical and methodological approach of the text, clarity and pertinence of the writing style are all aspects of the evaluation by the referees.
  • The content of the signed articles is the sole responsibility of the authors and does not express the opinion of the REnCiMa Editorial Team.
  • An interval of one year will be respected for the publication of articles of the same authorship and/or co-authorship.

 Regarding the rules for the preparation of the article, authors must observe the following:

  • Articles will be sent for evaluation only when prepared in the template, taking into account the guidelines contained therein.
  • The article must be written in between 15 and 25 pages, using an Arial font, size 12, space 1.5, and indentation of 1.25 cm in the first line.
  • The current ABNT standards for academic production must be followed.
  • The Scientific Council and the Ad hoc Referees Committee carry out an evaluation of the articles according to the guidelines available at this link.

  • Authors are authorized to assume additional contracts separately, for non-exclusive distribution of the version of the article published in the journal. Example: publishing in institutional repository or as a book chapter, with acknowledgment of authorship and initial publication in REnCiMa.

Privacy statement

User data reported in REnCiMa are used exclusively for the editorial services of this journal, and are not made available for any other purpose or third party.
