Basic information

Founded in 1999, ETD – Educação Temática Digital is a multidisciplinary electronic journal dedicated to the publication of scholarly articles from the national and international academic community. It publishes research of interest to the field of education and related areas, aiming to contribute to the training of researchers through the dissemination of studies and investigations linked to both national and foreign institutions.

ETD – Educação Temática publishes original works presented in the form of articles, dossiers, essays, experience reports, communications, literature reviews, and book reviews. Interviews may occasionally be published.

Texts are accepted in Portuguese, English, or Spanish, and may be published in all three languages, with translation or versioning costs borne by the authors.

Approved manuscripts undergo grammatical revision by a qualified professional. The costs of this revision are the responsibility of the authors. Publication is digital, and articles are made available exclusively in the online version on the journal’s platform and other indexing databases.

ETD – Educação Temática Digital does not charge submission or publication fees. The online version is open access and free of charge.

The abbreviated title of the journal is ETD – Educ. Temat. Digit., which should be used in footers and bibliographic references.

ETD – Educação Temática Digital was rated A1 in the current four-year CAPES Qualis evaluation.
