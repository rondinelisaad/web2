Basic information

Revista Educação PUC-Campinas is published three times a year, edited by the Post-Graduate Program in Education, Center for Applied Human and Social Sciences of the Pontifícia Universidade Católica of Campinas. It is a journal for the posting of studies and scientific, theoretical or empirical research, in the area of ​​Education; it is aimed at the dissemination of knowledge and the fostering of academic debate on relevant topics in the national and international educational scenario to help understanding the educational phenomenon today.

Articles submitted for publication must be unpublished, in the print or electronic media, and their simultaneous submission for evaluation in another journal is not allowed. The journal accepts articles for publication written in Portuguese, Spanish and English, in Continuous Publication (CP) mode and with a thematic section.

The Thematic Section is intended for the publication of articles coordinated with each other, from different authors and institutions, dealing with a topic of current interest.

The abbreviated title of the magazine is Rev. educ. PUC-Camp.
