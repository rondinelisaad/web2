Focus and scope

The periodical Cadernos de História da Educação is connected with the area of History of Education, covering a series of themes in this area, which include History of School Institutions, History of School Subjects, History of Educational Concepts, the Press and History of Education, Historiography of Education, etc. The periodical aims to publish the results of studies and research of a scientific nature carried out by Brazilian and international researchers linked to the theme of History and Historiography of Education, as well as promote exchange of ideas and of new knowledge among Brazilian and international researchers of academic-scientific institutions dedicated to investigation in the field of History and Historiography of Education.
