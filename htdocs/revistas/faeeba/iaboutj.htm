Focus and scope

The Revista da FAEEBA - Educação e Contemporaneidade - is a quarterly, thematic journal published by the State University of Bahia (Department of Education/Graduate Program in Education and Contemporaneity). It publishes scientific articles in the field of Education. The Revista da FAEEBA's main objective is to publish original scientific articles. The Revista da FAEEBA accepts scientific articles (academic texts that communicate research findings, including an introduction, theoretical basis, methodology, results, and conclusion) and essays (exceptionally, at the editors' discretion). These documents are submitted in a continuous flow, and the journal's rules and guidelines for its various sections (thematic dossier, studies, and interviews) must be followed. The journal publishes articles in Portuguese, English, French, Italian, and Spanish.
