About the journal

 

Basic information

Ensaio: Avaliação e Políticas Públicas em Educação is a publication of the Cesgranrio Foundation whose purpose is to publish articles related to Education, with the theme of questions on evaluation and public policies in education, prioritizing the results of research, theoretical studies and essays.

Articles submitted to Ensaio magazine, must, necessarily, follow the editorial line of the magazine and meet the rules established for publication.

The ENSAIO magazine only accepts new contributions, and the articles can be presented in Portuguese, English or Spanish. It is understood that an article is unpublished when it has not been fully published in any national or foreign magazine, in the annals of congresses / seminars, in virtual spaces on the internet that have the purpose of disseminating scientific and academic works. It must still originate from scientific research in the area of ​​education. The magazine ENSAIO does not charge fees to authors.

The abbreviated title for bibliographic citations and footnotes is Ensaio: aval. pol. públ. Educ.
