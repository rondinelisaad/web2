Editorial policy and publication rules

Revista Trabalho Necessário (ISSN: 1808-799X) is an electronic publication, published every four months, by the Center for Studies, Documentation and Data on Work and Education – Neddate/UFF, linked to the Postgraduate Program in Education (Masters and Doctorate) and the Faculty of Education at the Fluminense Federal University. It is aimed at teachers and researchers, as well as undergraduate and postgraduate students, especially in the areas of Social Sciences and Humanities.

The Journal seeks to recover in its title one of the central concepts of Marxian thought, that is, work as a vital activity of the human species, understanding that the political struggle - theoretical and practical - for overcoming the commodity-producing society cannot be left aside. Ultimately, work will always be necessary.

The themes covered by Revista Trabalho Necessário they will be preferably linked to human formation and educational practices that take place at the institutional level or in movements and struggles that take place in different social spaces-times.

The editorial policy of Revista Trabalho Necessário is guided by the effort to understand and reconstruct, on a theoretical level, the different social mediations that constitute the relationship between the world of work and education, choosing historical materialism as a theoretical-methodological reference, guiding the critique of political economy, which leads to a historical view of class society. By seeking in phenomena the multiple determinations or mediations that constitute them, relating part-whole, subject-object or subjectivity-objectivity, past-present, within a historical totality, historical materialism opposes itself to idealist, empiricist, structural-functionalist references , post-structuralist and post-modern that also analyze capitalism in different ways.

In this sense, the editorial line of Revista Trabalho Necessário considers that, in societies divided into antagonistic social classes, the production of knowledge is always linked to a certain conception of reality. It follows from this that, in the permanent dispute over reading the meaning of reality, the mere sum of theories becomes impossible, as eclecticism intends. Obviously, however, as Gramsci suggests, incorporations of aspects of the theories in dispute may occur, which contribute to understanding reality in the broadest possible way. Finally, it clarifies that it is open to the appreciation of texts that are based on theoretical-methodological references that diverge from its editorial line, as long as they demonstrate an internal coherence in the ontological and epistemological scope, and that stimulate a fruitful debate in the critical relationship with this editorial policy.

 

General instructions

The Journal publishes Thematic Issues organized by research groups associated with the Center for Studies, Documentation and Data on Work and Education – Neddate, considering their affinity with the magazine's editorial line. As a means of conveying and mediating critical thinking in relation to the world of work, human formation and the historical relationships between work and education, the Thematic Issues will be composed of articles by invited authors and articles sent spontaneously, all unpublished. In a continuous flow, TN will continue to receive articles with other themes. All texts must comply with the magazine's publication standards and the deadlines established by the notices, for subsequent evaluation.

The publication of articles (thematic number and other themes), essays and reviews is subject to two opinions issued by members of the Scientific Committee, the Editorial Board and/or ad hoc collaborators. In case of discrepancies, a third opinion will be requested. Specific problems will be evaluated and decided by the Editors. For the Homage, Classics, Interview, Theses / Dissertations (expanded summary) and Memoirs and Documents sections, the texts will receive evaluation and approval by the Journal's editors in conjunction with members of the Scientific Committee, the Editorial Board or Organizers of the Thematic Issues who invited the author(s) to compose the respective sections.

The selection of works for publication, in accordance with the evaluation form, takes as basic criteria its relevance and relevance to the Journal's editorial line, the consistency and rigor in the theoretical approach, methodology and argumentation, the dialogue with Marxist production, as well as the correctness and clarity in the construction of the text.

The selection process or estimated time from submission to publication of the text will correspond to the period from 04 to 06, and may be expanded due to force majeure.

The result of the submission process will be made by Editorial Decision, corresponding to evaluation by double-blind peers or by the editors, and will be communicated to the authors by email. The entire process can also be monitored by the OJS System.

The result of the submission process, according to the evaluation form and the OJS system, can be: a) Accept for publication in this form (requiring the inclusion of pre-textual data); b) Accept for publication with mandatory corrections (in terms of linguistic revision, number of characters, modifications suggested by reviewers, in addition to inclusion of pre-textual data); c) Submit to a new round of evaluation (requires mandatory corrections and substantive modifications indicated by the reviewers who requested them); d) Submit to another journal (not in line with the focus and scope of the journal); e) Do not accept (the manuscript is not in a condition to be published).

When pre-evaluating texts, editors reserve the right not to accept works that deviate from editorial policy and publication guidelines and standards, as per instructions to authors.
Editors reserve the right to suggest and make changes to the original texts with the aim of maintaining the homogeneity and quality of the publication. Likewise, they reserve the right to cancel the publication of texts whose final review indicates the need for adjustments that have not been made by the authors within the specified period.

 

Instrucions for preparing manuscripts

Works can be submitted in Portuguese or Spanish and, exceptionally, in another language.

Texts cannot be submitted or evaluated simultaneously by other journals; must be original and unpublished and, preferably, resulting from research.

Articles must have a minimum of 30 thousand and a maximum of 60 thousand characters with spaces, including bibliographic references and notes.

Essays must have a minimum of 15 thousand and a maximum of 30 thousand characters with spaces, including bibliographical references and notes. These may take the form of text or a photo essay, mixing images and text.

Articles and essays in text format must present a summary of up to 600 characters with spaces, and three to five keywords.

Reviews must have a minimum of 7 thousand and a maximum of 10 thousand characters with spaces. They are intended for the presentation, examination and judgment of recent work (no more than 1 year from the last edition of the magazine). It must contain a technical positioning regarding the object of analysis, followed by a summary of the content and possible demonstration of its importance for the field of studies and research on Work-Education. Formatting follows the same guidelines for articles, having to contain the complete bibliographic reference of the work reviewed.

The Homage, Classics and Interviews sections will be the responsibility of the editors, and may be suggested by the Editorial Board, Scientific Committee and Organizers of Thematic Issues. They cannot exceed 30 thousand characters with spaces. There is no need to present abstracts.

Theses and Dissertations will be nominated by the editors, Editorial Board, Scientific Committee and organizers of the Thematic Issue. Expanded abstracts must have a minimum of 7 thousand and a maximum of 10 thousand characters with spaces, including keywords, bibliographic references and notes.

Researchers from the Work-Education field and other fields of knowledge will be invited to the Memory and Documents section. The texts will be evaluated by the editors. They must have a minimum of 7 thousand and a maximum of 10 thousand characters, with space. The text presented must be separate from the document analyzed by the author.

Manuscripts must be sent exclusively online, and the author(s) must register in the Journal's system Revista Trabalho Necessário (OJS). Other communication may be made via email revistatrabalhonecessario@gmail.com, if necessary.

At the time of submission, all the authors' identification data (a maximum of three) must be filled in in the appropriate fields on the registration page (full name, highest degree with the institution and country in full, with the respective acronyms), institutional affiliation (in full and with the acronyms), areas of evaluation or studies, telephone number, email to contact the editors, ORCID number, Curriculum Vitae (in Lattes format, for Brazilian authors).

The author(s), a maximum of three, must submit the text of the article without identification of authors and without information about the author(s). The information will be filled in after the result of the evaluation process of the submitted article.

The text must undergo specialized linguistic review and be in accordance with the ABNT (NBR 6023/2018-Revised and NBR 10520/2017) contained in the Template provided. Submissions that do not comply with the standards will be returned to the authors.

Editors reserve the right not to accept works that deviate from editorial policy and publication guidelines and standards, as per instructions to authors. Editors reserve the right to suggest and make changes to the original texts with the aim of maintaining the homogeneity and quality of the publication. Likewise, they reserve the right to cancel the publication of texts whose final review indicates the need for adjustments that have not been made by the authors, in accordance with the opinions, within the specified period. Sending any collaboration automatically implies the full transfer of copyright to the Revista Trabalho Necessário.

 

Guidelines for formatting texts

Works must be in A4, MS Word, Arial 12 format, justified, line spacing 1.5 cm, without space between paragraphs and with an indentation of 1.25 cm at the beginning of each paragraph.

The margins: left and top must be 3 cm and the bottom and right must be 2.0 cm.

The work must contain title, abstract and keywords in Portuguese, Spanish and English.

The title in Portuguese must be in Arial 12, centered, in bold, capital letters, 1.5 spacing, with up to 120 characters, with space. After the title, single-space the author's name.  If there are 2 or 3 authors, the names must be separated by single spacing.

The summary in Portuguese must be in Arial 10.

Titles in Spanish and English must be in Arial 10, with left alignment, bold, capital letters and single spacing. It should be below the summary in Portuguese. The Spanish-language summary is second in order, after the Portuguese-language summary and lastly the English-language summary. The respective abstracts and keywords must be in Arial 10, single spaced.

The titles of sections and subsections in Arial 12, in bold, upper and lower case, aligned to the left margin, with single spacing between lines, and 1.5 cm spacing between the section titles and subsections and the text. For highlighting, within the text, use only italics.

The final version of the approved texts must contain, in a footnote, the following information, in Arial 10, single spacing between lines, justified: name of the author(s), title (institution and country in full, with the respective acronyms), institutional affiliation (in full and with acronyms), areas of evaluation or studies, email, ORCID and Lattes link.

Footnotes must be explanatory. All notes must be numbered and appear at the bottom of the page, with Arial 10 font, single spacing, justified. For footnotes, long citations, to indicate the source (authorship) of figures and tables or authorship information, use Arial 10 font, single spacing, justified. For captions and content of illustrations and tables, use Arial font, size 10.

Citations throughout the text must follow the form: Author (date) or (Author, date), Example: Marx (1977), or (Mészáros, 2002).  Literal citations must be enclosed in quotation marks and follow the form: Author, (date, p. xx) or (Author, date, p. xx). Different titles by the same author published in the same year must be differentiated by adding a letter after the date both in the text and in the list of references, according to the example: (Gramsci, 1995a), (Gramsci, 1995b).

Literal quotations must be inserted in the paragraph in quotation marks. The quotation marks in the original text must be replaced by single quotation marks. Literal quotations with more than three lines must be highlighted from the text (one enter before and one after), with a 4 cm indentation to the left, single space, Arial 11 font. They must follow the format: (Author, date, p. xx ). To indicate insertions of fragments in the original text or deletions of part of the original text, brackets [ ] must be used.

References must only contain the authors, texts and documents cited in the work. They are presented, at the end of the text, in alphabetical order, with single space, following the updated ABNT standards, according to the link provided above.  Detailed examples can be found in the Template (or in the submissions tab)       In references by the same author, with different works or as co-authorship, the author's name must be repeated, instead of using _________.  (Using ___________ does not allow recording in citation metrics, which interferes with h-Index ranking).

 

Privacy policy

The names and addresses provided in this magazine will be used exclusively for the services provided by this publication, and will not be made available for other purposes or to third parties.
