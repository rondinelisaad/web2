About the journal

 

About the journal

Revista Trabalho Necessário is a quarterly publication from the Center for Studies, Documentation and Data on Work and Education - NEDDATE, linked to the Postgraduate Program in Education and the Faculty of Education of the Universidade Federal Fluminense – UFF. Publishes results of research and studies on the world of work, human formation and historical relationships between work and education. It is aimed at teachers, researchers and undergraduate and postgraduate students, especially in the areas of social sciences and humanities.

The Journal seeks to recover in its title one of the central concepts of Marxian thought, that is, work as a vital activity of the human species, understanding that the political struggle - theoretical and practical - for overcoming the commodity-producing society cannot be left aside . Ultimately, work will always be necessary.

The themes covered by Revista Trabalho Necessário they will be preferably linked to human formation and educational practices that take place at the institutional level or in movements and struggles that take place in different social spaces.

The editorial policy of Revista Trabalho Necessário is guided by the effort to understand and reconstruct, on a theoretical-practical level, the different social mediations that constitute the relationship between the world of work and education, choosing historical materialism as a theoretical-methodological reference, guiding the critique of political economy, which leads to a historical view of society. By seeking in phenomena the multiple determinations or mediations that constitute them, relating part-whole, subject-object or subjectivity-objectivity, past-present, within a historical totality, historical materialism opposes itself to idealist, empiricist, structural-functionalist references, post-structuralist and post-modern that also analyze capitalism in different ways.

A Revista Trabalho Necessário considers that, in societies divided into antagonistic social classes, the production of knowledge is always linked to a certain conception of reality. It follows from this that, in the permanent dispute over reading the meaning of reality, the mere sum of theories becomes impossible, as eclecticism intends. Obviously, as Gramsci suggests, incorporations of aspects of critical theories can occur, which contribute to understanding reality in the broadest possible way. Finally, it clarifies that it is open to the appreciation of texts that are based on theoretical-methodological references that diverge from its editorial policy, as long as they demonstrate an internal coherence in the ontological and epistemological scope, and that stimulate a fruitful debate in the critical relationship with this editorial line. Journal history

Between 1985 and 2002, the forms of socialization of collective knowledge produced within Neddate/PPGEd/FEUFF occurred through the Study Series – Neddate and its Newsletter, the organization of a Collection on work and education, in addition to seminars , extension projects, book publication, theoretical-methodological discussions and other activities that continue today.  Considering the importance of innovating technologically, with the aim of expanding the forms of socialization, dissemination and dialogue of academic production at national and international level, the Revista Trabalho Necessário (ISSN: 1808-799X) began to be organized in October 2002 by an Operational Committee (OC) composed of Ronaldo Rosas, Francisco da Silveira Lobo Neto, José Rodrigues and José Luiz Cordeiro Antunes. From there, the general aspects of its Editorial Policy, approved by the permanent members of NEDDATE, are discussed and listed. Its first issue was published in 2003, already in online format, as an electronic periodical.

Between 2003 and 2007, the magazine was published annually; from 2008 to 2014 its periodicity became biannual;  As of 2015, its publication became quarterly.

The continuous editing and publishing processes, without interruption or delay, were led by professors Gaudêncio Frigotto and Maria Ciavatta, with several editors/teachers throughout their 21 years: Ronaldo Rosas, José Rodrigues, Francisco José da Silveira Lobo Neto, Luciana Requião, Sonia Rummert, Jaqueline Ventura, Lia Tiriba, Maria Cristina Paulo Rodrigues, José Luiz Cordeiro Antunes, Jacqueline Botelho and Regis Arguelles, who worked hard for their academic recognition. José Luiz Cordeiro Antunes, who served as editor between 2018 and 2023, is currently an assistant editor/collaborator. In 2024, the editors are Lia Tiriba (editor-in-chief, since 2018), Jacqueline Botelho (since 2022) and Adriana Barbosa (since 2024).

From 2018 onwards, the Magazine began publishing Thematic Issues, with organizers belonging to research groups associated with the Center for Studies, Documentation and Data on Work and Education – Neddate (from the CNPq Directory), considering their theoretical affinities -methodological with the magazine's editorial line. The Thematic Number is made up of articles by invited authors and articles received by spontaneous demand. It has the sections Homage, Classic Text, Interview, Memoir and Documents, Review, Essay, Theses and Dissertations, preferably linked to the theme in question. It also has Articles on Other Themes, in a continuous flow.

Each group of editors printed the editorial work in a peculiar way, within its objective conditions, respecting the procedures agreed both internationally and nationally, as well as in the UFF Periodical Editors' Forum, without, however, violating the conception of the magazine's editorial policy. It is important to remember that between 2018-2022 we increased the number of indexers, updated Google Analytics and its respective reports in terms of metrics.

In line with the principles of Open Science, one of the challenges of the Journal is to expand scientific dissemination, in order to favor the democratization of knowledge. To this end, in addition to the YouTube channel and the Neddate website (https://neddate.sites.uff.br/), we believe it is essential to disseminate information about activities and preliminary research results on social networks (Facebook and Instagram) , with the aim of reaching a larger audience, which can allow us to expand relations between universities, popular social movements and society in general.

In addition to Latin American researchers, with whom we have had fruitful dialogues (see n. 36, n. 46 and 47), we want to deepen the dialogue with Ibero-American, African and Anglo-Saxon researchers about the world of work and the work-education nexus. Perhaps, in the near future, it could become a bilingual magazine (Portuguese-Spanish), with a view to consolidating international relations, both through language proximity and cultural ties.

Regarding ethics, it is important to note that the peer review process is double-blind in the evaluation of articles, with the participation of a set of reviewers from the scientific and ad hoc committee, respecting the geographic distribution (national and international) of the articles. institutions in different regions of Brazil. We also seek to monitor innovations and changes in the editorial debate in the management of academic journals, including the possibility of peer review, in a semi-open manner. Another important aspect related to the Journal's innovation is joining indexing bases that assist in the internationalization process and the discussion of open sciences, such as Scopus, Scielo, Web of Science and Redalyc.

Dialogue with some research groups in the Work-Education field, present mainly in GT 09 of ANPEd, has been fundamental. It was from discussions at INTERCRÍTICA - National Exchange of Research Groups in Work-education that the creation of thematic issues (2018) emerged as a proposal, considering their affinity with our editorial line: historical materialism, in dialogue with other theoretical references. Without disregarding the importance of other magazines on Work-education, and following the editorial line established in 2003, our intention has been to sensitize other research groups about the importance of our electronic magazine as a means of conveying and mediating critical thinking in relation to to the world of work, human formation and the historical relationships between work and education, according to NEDDATE's lines of research.  In 2025, we will continue dialogue with Anped's GT 09 - Work-Education, as well as with other groups present at Anped, due to theoretical-methodological proximity. Among them, we mention GTs 05 - Educational Policy, GT 18 - Youth and Adult Education and others, such as GT03 - Social movements, subjects and educational processes, GT21 - Education and Ethnic-Racial Relations, GT22 - Environmental Education. To this end, we will carry out a consultation with these groups for the purpose of organizing the 2025 editions of the Revista Trabalho Necessário.

It is important to note that we will also continue with the quarterly publication, raising the possibility of becoming continuous publication (PC), depending on the Universidade Federal Fluminense favoring a technology that guarantees the materiality of our Editorial Policy. Meeting the foundations of historical and dialectical materialism, we want to reaffirm an ethical-political and technical-productive perspective that promotes the internal unity of the thematic number and the sections that compose them, in their entirety. In summary, reaffirming its editorial policy and the Open Science perspective, Revista Trabalho Necessário is a publication by NEDDATE, a research group created in 1985. It is a multidisciplinary journal that works with research groups in Work-Education in Brazil and that, since 2018, have become organizers of thematic issues, together with other national and international groups in the areas of human and social sciences. In May 2023, we celebrate the 20th anniversary of Revista Trabalho Necessário; In 2025 we will publish our 50th edition, which will also be a reason for great joy. There are 21 years of experience in conveying scientific knowledge committed to defending public education, against social injustices, defending democracy as a political system of access to the goods necessary for a dignified life and facing social crises (hunger, wars, migrations) and climate change that devastates the planet and threatens the survival of humanity.

 

Mission

Revista Trabalho Necessário - ISSN 1808-799X is a quarterly publication of the Center for Studies, Documentation and Data on Work and Education - NEDDATE, linked to the Graduate Program in Education and the Faculty of Education of the Fluminense Federal University - UFF. From a multidisciplinary perspective, it publishes the results of research and studies on the world of work, human formation and the historical relationship between work and education. It is aimed at teachers, researchers and undergraduate and postgraduate students, especially in the social sciences and humanities.

 

Fees for submission and publication

Revista Trabalho Necessário (TN - ISSN: 1808-799X) does not charge any fees for texts submitted for evaluation by its reviewers, nor does it make any charges related to the publication process (review, layout, printing and distribution). As it is a free access periodical, no fee is also charged for access or download.

As supporters of Open Science, we are against any and all types of charges for authors (submission and publication) and readers (access or subscription). We understand that the costs of the magazine are the responsibility of public institutions, as they are maintained by Brazilian citizens.

For its sustainability, we are in favor of claiming public resources from the UFF Journal Editors' Forum, the Postgraduate Program in Education, the Faculty of Education and funding institutions in general.
