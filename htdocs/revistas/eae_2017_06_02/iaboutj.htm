Mission


  

Publishes unpublished works directly or indirectly related to the issue of educational assessment, in the form of articles, research reports, theoretical-methodological essays and reviews.

 

About the Jornal

 

Quarterly journal created in 1990 as a successor of the Educação e Seleção (1980-1989). It publishes unpublished works directly or indirectly related to the issue of educational assessment, in the form of articles, research reports, theoretical-methodological essays and reviews.

This journal provides immediate open-access to its content, following the principle that making free scientific knowledge available to the public provides greater global democratization of knowledge.
