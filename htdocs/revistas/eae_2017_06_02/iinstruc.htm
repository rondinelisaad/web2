Instructions to authors

 

Estudos em Avaliação Educacional  is a quarterly journal that publishes original papers, in online and printed versions, related to educational assessment, presented in the form of articles, research reports, theoretical-methodological essays and reviews that are submitted exclusively to the journal. In exceptional cases, papers from foreign journals (with source indication), which will have to go through the same process for the evaluation of unpublished articles, are accepted. The authors are required to submit permission from the magazine in which their article was originally published.

Articles are published in Portuguese and Spanish. Papers submitted in other languages are translated into Portuguese.

 

Paper evaluation

The articles received are initially evaluated by the Editorial Committee. Those which do not fall within the scope of the journal are returned to the authors; those that comply with the criteria of the journal are forwarded to two specialists: external evaluators, who are either members of the Editorial Board or researchers from the Carlos Chagas Foundation, with a PhD and affiliated with institutions other than those of the authors. The evaluation is conducted according to the following criteria: theme relevance to the study area; theoretical-methodological consistency; relevance and timeliness of bibliography; clarity in presenting the object of study and argumentation; accuracy in the use of concepts; relevance, clarity and the basis of the statistical methodology employed. In the evaluation process, the names of the reviewers and authors remain confidential. In the last issue of each volume, the names of all reviewers, who collaborated with the magazine throughout the year, are published.

The authors receive communication relating to experts’ opinions, stating whether their text was accepted, rejected or if it needs to be revised for publication. The Editorial Committee reserves the right to refuse to publish the article if the corrections requested do not meet the standards set.

 

Paper Publication

If the article is accepted for publication, the journal may make small formal modifications in the text, while respecting the style and the opinion of the authors. The articles must not be published in any other form within six months of their publication in Estudos em Avaliação Educacional. Articles by the same author will only be published with an interval of at least six months.

 

Authorship

The authors must submit for publication: full name (without abbreviations); institutional affiliation or last professional occupation; city, state and country of the institution; education (graduate, specialist, master/master’s candidate, doctor/doctoral candidate), address, telephone, mobile phone and email contact. It is also requested that authors indicate how they want their names to appear on the publication and which email should be used. The authors will receive three printed copies of the journal in which their articles are published.

The copyright of the published articles are reserved for Estudos em Avaliação Educacional, under a Creative Commons license. The views expressed in the texts are the responsibility of the authors.

For publication of material in other media, contact: eae@fcc.org.br.

 

Paper Submission

To submit an article to Estudos em Avaliação Educacional, it is necessary to register in the Portal of Journals of the Carlos Chagas Foundation:  http://publicacoes.fcc.org.br/ojs/, creating a login and password. The author should access this system, click on the link "New submission" and fill in the necessary information in five steps:

Step 1. Start submission: fill in the fields: Section; Submission Language; Conditions for submission (all items in this field must be selected); Copyright statement; and Comments to the editor (if there are any).

Step 2. Transfer of the manuscript: submit the article, in PDF, for evaluation. This version of the article should not contain any references that will identify the author. Thus, it is necessary to remove the authors’ names from the article as well as the names of institutions and projects, or even any mention thereof in the sources of tables/graphs/charts/figures or bibliographic reference.

Step 3. Submission metadata (Indexing): inclusion of authors’ information, as well as the title and the abstract of the article submitted, collaborators and funding agencies (if any) and the references of the text.

Step 4. Transfer of additional documents: articles must be submitted in Microsoft Word, with all authorship information. At this stage of submission, charts, graphs, tables and figures should be supplied as separate, editable files.

Step 5. Confirmation of submission: at this stage, the author must click on “Finish submission”.

To track the status of the submission, the author must access the system → menu → access → login and password. It is recommended that all authors are registered in the system. However, it is possible to send the text with the registration of only one of the authors.

All documents sent to Estudos em Avaliação Educacional must be in the following format: upper margin, 3 cm; lower margin, 3 cm; left margin, 3 cm; and right margin, 2 cm; paragraph indentation, 1.25; line spacing, 1.5; without space (before or after) between paragraphs; pages numbered (after the cover page, on the bottom right); font size: Times New Roman, 12 points in the body.

Articles, research reports and theoretical-methodological essays: cannot exceed 25 pages (including attachments).

Reviews: must be maximum 6 pages, containing the reference to the review, title and critical analysis that brings contributions to the field of study of the article.

 

● Titles and subtitles: maximum of 11 words (including articles, prepositions, conjunctions, etc.). All material, except for reviews, must come with an abstract in Portuguese containing a maximum of 11 lines, stating the objective, method, results and conclusions. The lines must be single-spaced with no space between paragraphs, also without acronyms or references. It must contain, at the beginning, the title of the article. At the end of the abstract, four keywords (descriptors) of the content of the text should be provided.

 

● Quotes, references, notes and acronyms must comply with ABNT rules (NBR 10520, 2002). Direct citations (text), up to three lines, should be incorporated into the text, between quotation marks, and must indicate the author's surname, year and page number.

Ex.: Em função desses indicadores, “chegou-se à organização de cinco grandes grupos de escolas denominadas azul, verde, amarelo, laranja e vermelho” (SÃO PAULO, 2001, p. 55).

 

● Quotes with more than three lines should be presented without quotation marks, 1.25 cm indentation from the left margin, single-spaced between lines and with no space between paragraphs, in Times New Roman font, size10 in the body, with author’s surname, year and page. Ex.:

[...] a sofisticação técnica da avaliação nacional – que hoje ocupa a atenção da cúpula decisória e de seus assessores – apresenta-se como entrave para a compreensão; tanto pelos atores dos sistemas e escolas como pela população em geral, do processo avaliativo realizado. (FREITAS, 2004, p. 685)

 

● For quotes-in-quotes the Latin phrase “apud” (quoted by) should be used to identify the source that was effectively consulted, which should have the full reference in the footer. In the references list, only the works consulted should be included (CARONE et al., 2003).

Ex.: Para Watson (apud CARONE et al., 2003) [...].

 

● Indirect Bibliographical references should be incorporated into the text in parentheses (year). Ex.: Segundo João Barroso (2006), todos...

 

● Explanatory notes should be avoided and used only when it is strictly necessary, preferably, without exceeding three lines. They must always appear in the page footer, sequentially numbered.

 

● Acronyms must be explained when first mentioned in the article. Ex.: Exame Nacional do Ensino Médio (Enem).

 

● The statistical methodology employed should be described in detail so that results can be replicated by a reader with experience in the subject, who has access to the original data. On the other hand, the use of overly technical language should be avoided, in order to make the methodological description clear enough for a non-specialist reader to understand it. These recommendations result mainly in procedures such as:

-          provide details about the experimental units, randomization and sampling procedures;

-          describe and/or provide access to research collection tools;

-          indicate all descriptive and inferential techniques used, providing bibliographical references whenever needed;

-          present the quantitative results with their respective measures of uncertainty (confidence intervals, p-values, etc.);

-          adopt a level of precision in presentation of data and results (for example, number of decimal places) standardized and consistent with the research possibilities;

-          use graphics and tables, whenever appropriate, to facilitate the organization and summary of the data and understanding of the results;

-          discuss the reasonableness of results and report on possible limitations of the methods employed;

-          define technical terms, mathematical symbols and acronyms used in the article;

-          specify the statistical software used.

 

● Tables, graphs, charts and figures (as well as titles and sources) should be presented in the body of the text, and not in text boxes; aligned left, on their corresponding page, numbered in Arabic numerals, with titles (placed above, in font size 12 ) standardized to the format and terms used. Below these, not in text boxes, it is necessary to indicate the data source (referring to the bibliographical references), with authorship and year, including those elaborated by the authors themselves, in font size 10, aligned left, with 1.5 spacing between lines. Tables, graphs and charts must be in software compatible with the Windows environment, preferably in Excel, and figures (illustrations, images, maps, photos, etc.) in high resolution files (300 dpi), all produced in black and white, no larger than 10 cm  in width.

 

● Bibliographical references should come at the end of the text in alphabetical order by author’s last name and, whenever possible, the first name of the authors should be provided in full. Titles of the articles should be presented in italics. When there are two or three authors, the first author should be separated from the others by a semicolon; if there are more than three authors, the main author is mentioned (referenced in the text) and the others are replaced by the words “et al.”.  The presentation should follow the ABNT rules (NBR 6023, 2002). The accuracy of the references and the correct citation in the text are the author’s responsibility, and are required for publication of the article.

RIGHT OF REPLY

Estudos em Avaliação Educacional welcomes comment(s) to articles published in the journal. If the comment is accepted for publication, the journal will offer the author the same space for reply, which can be published in the same issue as the comment or in the subsequent issue. Both are subject to the same evaluation process as other texts. Comments or replies to reviews are not accepted.
