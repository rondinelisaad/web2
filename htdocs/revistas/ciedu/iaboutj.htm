Basic information

Ciência & Educação is published three times a year, with editions in April, August and December and its purpose is the dissemination of original scientific articles in areas of Science and Mathematics Education, and related subjects.

The journal abbreviated title is Ciênc educ. (Bauru), which should be used in bibliographies, footnotes, bibliographical notes and references.

 

Indexed in

Ciência & Educação is indexed in the following national and international databases:

  • BBE: Bibliografia Brasileira de Educação (INEP)
  • CLASE: Citas Latinoamericanas en Ciencias Sociales y Humanidades
  • DOAJ: Directory of Open Access Journals
  • EDUBASE (Unicamp)
  • IRESIE - Índice de Revistas de Educación Superior e Investigación Educativa
  • LATINDEX: Sistema Regional de Información en Línea para Revistas Científicas de América Latina, el Caribe, España y Portugal
  • Open J-Gate
