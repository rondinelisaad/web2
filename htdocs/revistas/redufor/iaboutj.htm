Focus and scope

The Educação & Formação journal (e-ISSN: 2448-3583), linked to the Graduate Programs in Education of the Universidade Estadual do Ceará. It publishes articles in the national and international educational field in the area of "Education", primarily in the field of "Teacher Education", considering that the target audience for the journal Educação & Formação is researchers in the area of education, teachers of basic and higher education, graduate students, and other professionals interested in these topics.

Educação & Formação is bilingual, edited online, and receives submissions in continuous flow, in Portuguese, English, Spanish, and French.

The journal requires a minimum doctoral degree for authors interested in submitting articles. In case of collective authorship, at least one of the authors must hold such title.

 

Periodicity

Continuous publication since 2020.
