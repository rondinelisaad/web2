Basic information


 Inter-Ação is a free quarterly publication of the Faculty of Education of the Federal University of Goiás and the Graduate Program in Education of FE/UFG. Its objective is to publish, through a double-blind review system involving ad hoc reviewers and members of the Scientific Council, unpublished works resulting from theoretical studies and research on education, covering the following lines of research: Education, work, and social movements; State, policies, and history of education; Culture and educational processes; Teacher training, professionalization, and educational practices; Fundamentals of the educational process.

 

Frequency

The journal is published in full format, with three issues per year: April, August, and December.
