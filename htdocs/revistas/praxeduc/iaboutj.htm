Mission

Práxis Educativa, published under the responsibility of the Graduate Program in Education (Master’s and Doctorate) - Universidade Estadual de Ponta Grossa, aims to publish manuscripts that contribute to its specific field of research and that can serve as a reference for other research works. The journal prioritizes the publication of manuscripts not published elsewhere and already concluded research.

 

Goals

Publishing papers and reviews that contribute to the debate in different fields in the area of Education.

 

Areas of interest

- Education
- Ethics and research in Education
- Contemporary Education Issues

 

History

Práxis Educativa was created in 2006 by the faculty of the Graduate Program in Education - Universidade Estadual de Ponta Grossa (PPGE/UEPG). Over time, the editorial team endeavored to index the journal on different national and international databases, to increase the number of manuscripts, improve the graphic quality, etc. Currently, it is a journal of continuous publication, which has published important articles by Brazilian and foreign authors, translations, reviews, interviews, documents.
