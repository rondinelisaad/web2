INSTRUCTIONS TO AUTHORS

  • Scope and policy
  • Model and preparation of manuscripts
  • Submission of manuscripts

 

Scope and policy

Práxis Educativa publishes original manuscripts not published elsewhere written by Brazilian and foreign authors. The theme of the articles must be related to the area of Education. The manuscripts must represent a relevant contribution to the specific field of investigation, and must present a consistent theoretical framework, clear and explicit arguments and language correction. The work needs to show that it has sufficient potential and consistency to serve as a reference for the work of other researchers. The journal prioritizes the publication of texts not published elsewhere and already concluded research. The journal also publishes reviews, interviews, documents. The manuscripts are evaluated by two reviewers (members of the Editorial Board or ad hoc reviewers). In case of divergence, a third opinion will be requested or the editorial team will decide.

 

Model and preparation of manuscripts

The journal has the following sections: papers, reviews, interviews and documents.

Extension: Papers must have a maximum of 28 pages, in 1.5 spacing; lower and upper margins of 2.0 cm, and right and left margins of 2.5 cm; in A4 format. Reviews should have between 4 and 7 pages, with the same format, restricted to books published in the last 5 years.

Abstract: maximum of 10 lines (150 words), containing information about the objectives, methodological procedures, theoretical references and results. The abstract must present 3 (three) keywords. The abstract should be submitted in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

If the research has financial support from any institution, it must be mentioned.

References must follow APA Style - 7th edition, in the case of manuscripts written in other language than Portuguese.
Manuscripts in Portuguese, references must follow ABNT - NBR 6023:2018.

Authors who have published a manuscript must wait 3 (three) years for a new submission. Exceptional cases will be analyzed by the editorial team.

 

Submission of manuscripts

The manuscripts  must be submitted without identification of authorship, through the online submission system.

In the manuscript, any identifying information of authorship should be included.
