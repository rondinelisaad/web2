Scope and Policy

Novos Estudos publishes papers dealing with subjects of the humanities, debates, interviews, reviews and ficction written by brazilian and foreign authors. Since 1981, the journal has been published three times a year: in March, July and November. Texts sent to the editor are examined by the Editorial Board, that meets regularly to discuss each number, and by specialists ad hoc.

 

Manuscript Preparation

Articles and reviews should be no longer than eight thousand words (around 20 pages written in Times New Roman, size 12, espaço ½). The articles must bring a summary no longer than ten lines. Authors must inform their institutional, positions and titles. Each author receives 3 samples of the journal.

 

Sending of Manuscripts

 

Sending of manuscripts: novosestudos@cebrap.org.br
