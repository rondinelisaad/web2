Focus and scope

The Electronic Journal of Education is an initiative of the Graduate Program in Education at the Federal University of São Carlos. Its main objective is to publish articles, essays and dossiers on topics that contribute to the resurgence of the educational debate, as well as to the dissemination of knowledge produced in the area. It is aimed at researchers, professionals and students of Education. The journal is multilingual, however due to the site settings, abstract and keywords appear only in Portuguese, English and Spanish. Texts in languages other than Portuguese or Spanish may be translated and presented in the same edition.
