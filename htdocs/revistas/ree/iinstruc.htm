Instructions to authors

Verification items for submission

As part of the submission process, authors are required to verify submission compliance against all of the items listed below. Submissions that do not comply with the rules will be returned to the authors.

  • Complete registration of the authors on the REVEDUC homepage, with personal data, institutional affiliation, ORCID number and mini-curriculum, accompanied by the indication of areas of interest. Remember to tick the Reader and Author options. If the author has already registered, just access the system and start the five-step submission process.
  • Attachment of the article in Microsoft Word, OpenOffice or RTF: not identified according to the guidelines of the template available in the journal, following the guidelines for authors.
  • The contribution is original and unpublished, and is not being evaluated for publication by another journal; otherwise, it must be justified in "Comments to the editor".
  • URLs for references were provided where possible.
  • The text is single-spaced; uses 12-point Arial font; uses italics instead of underlining (except for URL addresses); figures and tables are inserted in the text, not at the end of the document in the form of attachments and strictly follow the Template (model) at the link: https://docs.google.com/document/d/16hjf2cBX4QqsKQWZU-gqIVdYrIJ-WK_5/edit
  • The text follows the ABNT style standards and bibliographic requirements described in Guidelines for Authors, which is just below this table.
  • Attaching a copy of the Copyspider report. Only articles with an index of no more than 3% of coincidence will be accepted, except in the cases of master's dissertations and doctoral thesis.
    Link: https://copyspider.com.br/main/pt-br/download
  • Copy of the Opinion that the research on which the article was based was submitted and approved by the Ethics Committee and that the participants signed the Free and Informed Consent Term indicating the process (when it comes to data collection involving human beings).
  • Declaration of Consent and Originality for the Submission, indicating that the article is original and has not been submitted for evaluation in another journal, and agreeing with the rules of submission, format, evaluation, approval and publication adopted by the journal, as well as.
    Statement Link: https://docs.google.com/document/d/14AW3Cmvsma8DI61aSJG9IVrCJznH4Qdu/edit
  • Declaration of the contribution of the authors in the research and in the manuscript when there is more than one author.
    Declaration Link: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gyXBc33-cQt50mPtc-bDV_nd1bxvXXJv/edit#heading=h.gjdgxs
  • P.S.: Submission is carried out in five steps. Follow the instructions in each step and, in the fourth, confirm the submission. Supplementary documents are inserted in the last step, for this, click on Revise this submission and insert the requested documents.

Guidelines for authors

Norms for publication: Electronic Journal of Education

See also the document Ethics and Disclaimer Policies adopted by REVEDUC

P.S.: to present your work, please strictly follow the Template available on the Journal's website.

The magazine is multilingual and texts in languages other than Portuguese or Spanish may be translated and presented in the same issue. In these cases, the name, contact email and Individual Taxpayer Registration of the reviewer and translator must appear in the endnote of the manuscript. It should be noted that in the case of translations, the cost of the Portuguese version is the responsibility of the authors.

Texts sent for publication must be submitted to the approval of members of the Journal's Internal and External Editorial Board. The opinions will be forwarded to the author, or to the authors for knowledge about their content and reformulation, if applicable. To learn more, see “Peer Review Process”.

Only texts derived from empirical or theoretical research carried out by researchers with at least a Master's degree will be accepted for analysis. Works by Master's students only in co-authorship with at least one Doctor.

The submission must be original and unpublished, and the author must declare that it is not being evaluated for publication by another journal; otherwise, it must be justified in "Comments to the editor".

Submission and publication of articles are free.

The following sections make up this journal:

  • Articles (Continuous Demand): reports of completed research, synthesis of original research;
  • Thematic dossiers: set of articles around a theme that expresses a contribution considered relevant to the area;
  • Essays: theoretical review, critical review of thematic bibliography or specific work;
  • Interviews: with researchers, theoretical authors and personalities who can contribute to discussions on relevant educational topics (only in Thematic Dossiers).

The following sections are retired in 2020: Image Analysis; Analysis of Didactic Material; Debates; Documents; Interviews (in continuous demand); Case studies; Great Authors of Education; Nominees; Reviews and Experience Reports.

Other sections may be created by the Journal's Editors.

1. THE TEXT
ATTENTION: For submissions, from 06/26/2019, it is mandatory to present the text to be evaluated following the Template.
The length of the text must be between 10 and 20 pages (not counting the references), Arial font, size 12, single spaced. The indentation for the beginning of each paragraph should be 1.5 cm from the margin. Margins: top and bottom 2.5 cm and left and right 3 cm. The submission must contain: title; abstract and keywords in Portuguese, English and Spanish. If the original language of the text is not Portuguese, it must also contain all these fields in the original language. The body of the text must contain: introduction, development items, final considerations and list of references in alphabetical order by authors' surnames.Figures should be inserted in the text and also sent according to the following guidelines:
Specifications for Reveduc images:

  1. Legible font, preferably sans serif, size at least 9pt.
  2. JPG format, resolution at least 300 dpi
  3. Avoid CAPS, shaded font, heavily condensed font

P.S.: For keywords in Portuguese, search the Thesaurus in education available at: http://pergamum.inep.gov.br/pergamum/biblioteca/pesquisa_thesauro.php?resolution2=1024_1
For keywords (descriptors in English), search the International Thesaurus on Education available at: http://eric.ed.gov/?ti=all

2 - SUBMISSION REGISTRATION

2.1 - The data of all authors (in the order they will be published) must be informed via the proper page of the REVEDUC-UFSCar environment at the time of submission: first name, last name, email, ORCID id, institution of affiliation, mini-curriculum with brief professional information (maximum 50 words).

2.2 - Titles, abstracts and keywords must be filled in in Portuguese, English and Spanish in the fields indicated at the time of submission, authors must also indicate the subjects and area of knowledge of the submitted manuscript.

2.3 - Summary (from 200 to 250 words) highlighting the objective, method and conclusions of the work. The text of the abstract, written in a single paragraph, using the verb in the third person, must be free of direct or indirect quotations, symbols or contractions that are not commonly used, and formulas and equations that are not absolutely necessary.

2.4 - Keywords (up to four words), which represent the content of the article (in the three languages already indicated);

2.5 - Indication of the funding or work promotion agency, when applicable.

2.6 - Titles and subtitles must contain a maximum of 15 words, including articles, prepositions, conjunctions, between others; they must be concise, clearly indicated (in the three languages already indicated).

Revista Eletrônica de Educação reserves the right to modify the text, when necessary, without harming its content, with the aim of standardizing the presentation, and may also reformulate the language, when necessary.

3. QUOTES
For quotations, follow the Standards of the Brazilian Association of Technical Standards - ABNT (NBR 10520/2002) updated in 2019.
Short literal quotes must be integrated into the text, between quotation marks, followed by parentheses with the author's surname, year and page of publication. Quotations with more than three lines must be presented with a 4 cm indent from the left margin, with a smaller font than the text (Arial, size 11, single space between lines) and without quotation marks. Within the parentheses, the author's surname, year and page of the publication from which they were taken must also be indicated.
Different titles by the same author, published in the same year, must be distinguished by the addition of lowercase letters, which must be in alphabetical order, immediately after the date and without spacing, for example: (OLIVEIRA, 1999a; OLIVEIRA, 1999b).

4. BIBLIOGRAPHICAL REFERENCES
Bibliographical references without literal citation must also be incorporated into the text in parentheses. Example: According to Silva (2003, p. 45).
P.S.: the inclusion of the page in these cases is not mandatory.

5. NOTES
The notes, sequentially numbered in Arabic numerals in the text, must appear at the end of each page, in the same order in which they appear in the text.

6. ACRONYMS
Acronyms must be unfolded when mentioned for the first time in the article.

7. BIBLIOGRAPHICAL REFERENCES
Bibliographical references must adhere to the works and authors cited in the text and must be presented in alphabetical order, according to ABNT NBR 6023/2018 standards, at the end of the work.
The author(s) are responsible for the correct and exact citation in the text and the corresponding information in the list of references at the end of the manuscript.

Conditions for publication

In order for accepted articles and essays to be published in REVEDUC, the authors must provide a grammatical review of the text and abstract (including them in a foreign language), by a professional with experience in the area, whose full name and Individual Taxpayer Registration must appear in the final note. The respective charges are the responsibility of the authors.

Privacy policy

The names and addresses informed in this magazine will be used exclusively for the services provided by this publication, not being made available for other purposes or to third parties.
